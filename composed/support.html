Support

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · Composed v1.4.23

Product stage

Composed is a free early beta. The app is usable, but you may find rough edges. Please tell us about them. You need your own Anthropic API key, and Anthropic charges you for what you use.

Supported platforms

  • macOS 12 (Monterey) or later — Apple Silicon and Intel (universal build)
  • Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)

iOS, Android, and browser access are not currently supported. Composed is a native desktop application — your data stays on your machine.

Automatic updates

Composed checks for updates when you open the app and asks before installing one. Updates are cryptographically signed — only official releases will install. The version number is shown at the bottom of the sidebar inside the app.

If an update fails to install, download the latest version directly from this page and run the installer — your data and settings are stored separately and will not be affected.

Known limitations

  • LinkedIn job pages cannot be scraped automatically due to LinkedIn's terms of service. Use Paste mode — copy the job description text and paste it into Composed.
  • Salary data is indicative and sourced from Adzuna's public API. It reflects advertised salaries and may not match your market or role level exactly.
  • AI quality depends on the detail in your CV and job description. The more specific your profile, the more targeted the output. Very long CVs (over ~5,000 words) may be partially truncated in some prompts.
  • Unsigned installers: macOS and Windows show a security warning the first time. The steps to continue are on the download section.
  • Internet connection required for all AI features, job search and salary lookup. CV editing and history browsing work offline.

Reporting an issue or sending feedback

Email harry@bemhatech.nl with:

  • A short description of what you expected and what happened instead
  • Your OS version (e.g. macOS 15.2 or Windows 11)
  • The Composed version number (shown at the bottom of the sidebar)

We aim to respond within 3 working days. Bug fixes are released as minor updates as soon as they are tested.

You can also use the Feedback button inside the Composed sidebar — it opens a pre-addressed email with your version number included.

Continuity

Composed is a founder-led product run by Harry Butcher (Netherlands). We will give at least 30 days' notice before discontinuing it.

Your data — what is stored locally

Everything you put into Composed is stored on your own computer, in the app's data folder:

  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/com.harrybutcher.composed/
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\com.harrybutcher.composed\

The folder holds your profile (name, CV text, stories, preferences and any API keys you add), your application history and drafts, and your outreach pipeline. Composed keeps a daily backup of your profile for seven days. Versions before 1.4.23 stored these files on your Desktop; the app moves them to the new folder.

These files are yours. You can back them up, inspect them or delete them. They are not uploaded to Bemhatech.

Your data — what is sent externally

Anthropic Claude API: When you use AI features (for example reading a job posting, matching and tailoring your CV, drafting letters and outreach, story interviews and interview preparation), the relevant text, including your CV and the job description, is sent to Anthropic. Requests go directly from the app to Anthropic using your own API key. They do not pass through any Bemhatech server. Your use is subject to Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

Other services the app contacts:

  • Job boards (only those you have switched on): Adzuna (using your own Adzuna keys, for jobs and salary figures) and Workable receive job titles and locations; Jobicy receives locations; RemoteOK and Arbeitnow are asked for their latest listings. For Gulf and African locations, Careerjet and Bayt receive job titles and locations. Your name and CV are not sent to job boards.
  • Job links you paste: the app opens that web page to read the posting.
  • Updates: the app checks bemhatech.nl for a new version.

As with any web request, these services can see your IP address. The Composed app sends no analytics, crash reports or usage tracking.
