Composed

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Overview

Composed is a native desktop application. Your CV, cover letters, job data, and application history are stored as files on your own machine. When you use AI features, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic using your own API key, as described in section 4.

2. Data Bemhatech collects

Bemhatech collects minimal data:

  • Past purchases: Composed is no longer sold. If you bought a licence in the past, Paddle processed the payment and shared your name and email address with us to deliver it. We do not store payment card details.
  • Support correspondence: If you contact us by email, we retain that correspondence for up to 2 years.
  • Website analytics: Our website may collect anonymised page-view data via Netlify's built-in analytics. No personal data is collected.

3. Data stored locally on your machine

All application data is stored on your own computer, in the app's data folder:

  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/com.harrybutcher.composed/
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\com.harrybutcher.composed\

The folder holds your profile (name, CV text, stories, preferences and any API keys you add), your application history and drafts, and your outreach pipeline. Composed keeps a daily backup of your profile for seven days. Versions before 1.4.23 stored these files on your Desktop; the app moves them to the new folder.

These files are yours. Bemhatech cannot access them. They survive app updates and can be deleted at any time by removing the files.

4. Data sent to third-party services

Anthropic Claude API: When you use AI features (for example reading a job posting, matching and tailoring your CV, drafting letters and outreach, story interviews and interview preparation), the relevant text, including your CV and the job description, is sent to Anthropic. Requests go directly from the app to Anthropic using your own API key. They do not pass through any Bemhatech server. Your use is subject to Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

Other services the app contacts:

  • Job boards (only those you have switched on): Adzuna (using your own Adzuna keys, for jobs and salary figures) and Workable receive job titles and locations; Jobicy receives locations; RemoteOK and Arbeitnow are asked for their latest listings. For Gulf and African locations, Careerjet and Bayt receive job titles and locations. Your name and CV are not sent to job boards.
  • Job links you paste: the app opens that web page to read the posting.
  • Updates: the app checks bemhatech.nl for a new version.

As with any web request, these services can see your IP address. The Composed app sends no analytics, crash reports or usage tracking.

5. Your API keys

Your Anthropic key (and Adzuna keys, if you add them) are stored in your profile file on your computer. Each is used only to sign requests to that service. They are never sent to Bemhatech.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data Bemhatech holds about you (limited to purchase records and support correspondence). To exercise these rights, email harry@bemhatech.nl. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

Purchase records are retained for as long as required by Dutch tax law (7 years). Support correspondence is retained for 2 years.
