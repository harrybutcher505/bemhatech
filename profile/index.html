Company Overview

Bemhatech is a software studio and operations consultancy based in Eindhoven, the Netherlands. It was founded to bring a rare combination to both software and operations work: extensive hands-on experience running fleet, warehouse and transportation operations, plus significant product leadership experience building the telematics software that runs those operations.

That combination shapes how Bemhatech works. Consultancy engagements and self-built products both follow the same pattern: identify a genuine operational opportunity, then deliver the solution through direct experience and applied innovation, not theory.
