Delivered Operations processing 40,000+ cases a week from the new facility.

PWC Kuwait

Opportunity Coordinate transport allocation and fleet control across Kuwait and the GCC.

Innovated The Micro Transport system, as product owner, and a Fleet Control Centre with a team of 26.

Delivered Centralised control of 7,000+ assets across the GCC and Iraq.

Agility (Regional MEA)

Opportunity Standardise fleet operations across multiple Middle East and Africa markets.

Innovated A "Fleet as a Service" delivery model, deployed starting in Kuwait, Bahrain, Abu Dhabi, Qatar, Saudi Arabia and Sudan.

Delivered TMS and fleet management best practice across 7,000+ assets region-wide.

Agility (Abu Dhabi)

Opportunity Reduce tyre and maintenance costs across a 180 vehicle fleet.

Innovated A Tyre Management Programme.

Delivered 20% cost reduction, fleet grew 31% at maintained efficiency.

Astrata (fmr. Qualcomm)

Opportunity Link driving behaviour directly to fuel cost, and give fleets a standard way into OEM truck data.

Innovated The Driver Coach fuel saving feature, alongside the first FMS OEM integrations.

Delivered Measurable fuel cost reduction for customers, and the start of a standards-based OEM integration practice.

Telogis / Verizon Connect

Opportunity Enterprise fleets lacked both optimised routing and a common way to integrate OEM truck data.

Innovated Route optimisation software at enterprise scale, and carried the FMS work through to the rFMS standard, contributing to it as part of the DOITS working group.

Delivered Standardised, scalable routing and OEM data integration for enterprise fleet customers.

Geotab

Opportunity Bring enterprise fleet data into the tools teams already used.

Innovated The Geotab Data Connector and Dashboard Insights platform.

Delivered Native Power BI and Tableau integration for enterprise clients.
